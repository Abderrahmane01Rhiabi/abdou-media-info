# Privacy Policy – AbdouMediaFinder **Last updated: November 3, 2026** ## 1. Introduction AbdouMediaFinder is a personal application developed and operated by an individual for private use. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when using the application. ## 2. Information Collection The application may access publicly available Pinterest content through the official Pinterest API, subject to the permissions granted by Pinterest. Depending on the API access granted, this may include: - Pinterest Pin information. - Image information and available image URLs. - Public content metadata. - Other information explicitly permitted by the Pinterest API. The application does not intentionally collect personal information from other Pinterest users beyond the information made available through authorized API access. ## 3. The information is not sold, rented, or shared with third parties for commercial purposes. ## 4. Data Storage The application runs locally on the developer's personal computer. Any information saved by the application is stored locally and is not intentionally made publicly accessible. ## 5.
