Privacy Policy

Application: AbdouMediaFinder

Last updated: November 3, 2026

1. Introduction

AbdouMediaFinder is a personal application developed and operated by an individual for private use. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when using the application.

2. Information Collection

The application may access publicly available Pinterest content through the official Pinterest API, subject to the permissions granted by Pinterest.

This information may include:

  • Pinterest Pin information.
  • Available image information and image URLs.
  • Public content metadata.
  • Other information explicitly permitted by the Pinterest API.

3. Use of Information

Information accessed through Pinterest is used exclusively for:

  • Personal visual research.
  • Discovering images and videos for personal editing projects.
  • Organizing media references.

The information is not sold, rented, or shared with third parties for commercial purposes.

4. Data Storage

The application runs locally on the developer's personal computer. Information saved by the application is stored locally and is not intentionally made publicly accessible.

5. Third-Party Services

The application uses Pinterest's official API and is subject to Pinterest's Privacy Policy and Developer Terms.

Pinterest Privacy Policy

6. Data Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect API credentials and locally stored information. Access tokens and application secrets are not intended to be shared publicly.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored information can be deleted by the application owner at any time.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application or its data-handling practices change.
